A note on ADR

Most commercial fights end in a room, not a courtroom.

A matter that runs three years through the High Court costs the client far more than the sum in dispute, and even a judgment has to be enforced. Where the parties will still need each other afterwards, or where the facts are messy and both sides are partly right, a negotiated settlement usually leaves the client in a better position than a win.

The chambers advises on which route a matter should take before any fees are committed, and appears as counsel in mediation and arbitration where that is the better road.

Negotiation

Structured settlement correspondence and meetings, with a written record of every term agreed.

Mediation

A neutral third party, a defined timetable, and a settlement that is documented and enforceable.

Arbitration

Where the contract provides for it, the matter is taken out of the court list entirely and decided by an arbitrator.
